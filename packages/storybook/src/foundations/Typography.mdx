# Typography

The system has four distinct voices. All font files are bundled in
`@a-novel-kit/uikit-fonts`; no browser request goes to a font CDN.

## Display

TeX Gyre Heros Condensed gives navigation landmarks and editorial headings a compact, recognizable
shape. Use it sparingly and prefer the real 400 or 700 faces.

<Typeset
  fontFamily={displayStack}
  fontSizes={[24, 32, 40, 48, 64]}
  fontWeight={700}
  sampleText="Build worlds worth returning to."
/>

## Interface

TeX Gyre Heros is the default product voice. Arimo and platform locale fonts complete scripts that
the Latin-only Heros files do not cover.

<Typeset
  fontFamily={interfaceStack}
  fontSizes={[12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 24, 32]}
  fontWeight={400}
  sampleText="Create, review, and publish your next chapter."
/>

## Story

Lora is reserved for narrative prose and long-form reading, keeping authored worlds visually
separate from the surrounding product chrome.

<Typeset
  fontFamily={storyStack}
  fontSizes={[16, 18, 20, 24, 32, 40]}
  fontWeight={400}
  sampleText="Beyond the last lantern, the road became a promise."
/>

## Monospace

JetBrains Mono is used for identifiers, structured values, logs, and code-adjacent content.

<Typeset fontFamily={monoStack} fontSizes={[12, 14, 16, 18, 20]} fontWeight={400} sampleText="scene.intensity = 0.72" />

## Weight rule

The interface and display families ship real regular and bold files. Use `400` or `700`; synthetic
intermediate weights undermine the intended typography and can vary by browser.
